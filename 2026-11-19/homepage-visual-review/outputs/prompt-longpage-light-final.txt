A single very tall vertical artboard for a developer-tool homepage: 1512×4200 px, aspect 1:2.78, read top to bottom as four stacked bands. Light edition, "paper and ink": warm paper, ink type, one drawn hairline that behaves like a plotter trace. Flat UI comp, crisp 2x detail, no browser chrome, no device mockup, no watermark, no visible section dividers. This brief is complete and self-contained.

GLOBAL
- Paper #F4F1E9 with 3% paper-fiber grain and a barely visible 2% warm falloff toward the lower-left corner. No gradients anywhere else. No haze, no vignette.
- Left editorial margin 72px; right content edge 1440px.
- One continuous 1.25px ink filament travels the entire 4200px height, drawn as #161815 with a 0.6px lighter core #3A3D38 that suggests a pen groove. Nothing on the page emits light: no glow, no bloom, no halo, no specular highlight. Depth is expressed only by opacity and stroke weight.
- One full-height printed column rule: 1px, #1A1C19 at 55% opacity, at x=907 (60% of the width), running from the very top edge of the canvas to the very bottom edge, crossing all four bands. It sits behind the type and behind the product card.
- The branch filament crosses this column rule deliberately. In band 1 both hero arcs pass through it; in bands 2 and 3 the horizontal exit lines cross it at y=1780, y=2160 and y=3000. Every crossing must be a clean 90°-style intersection of two 1px lines with no gap, no shadow and no highlight. The crossings are intentional and are part of the composition.
- Hierarchy comes from ink value, 1px rules and type scale only: no cards, no shadows, no blur. Section separation is achieved by 96px of empty paper, a 10px mono number, and the line's own 90° turns — nothing else.
- Type scale is fixed in absolute pixels and must NOT scale with the canvas height: display 148, section display 64, section heading 28, body 15, mono 10. Chinese display face with squarish terminals (MiSans-like); technical mono for numbers and hashes.
- One accent only: deep green #0E5B37, used as position or state — two characters in the headline, one filled square in the tree, one terminal disc. All other color is paper, ink and grey.
- No vertical stretching of elements. Bands are composed, not stretched.

PALETTE: paper #F4F1E9, card #FBFAF6, ink #101210, secondary #4A5049, tertiary #6E726A, muted #9A9D94, rule-soft #E4E1D8, rule #C9C6BC, rule-strong #161815, accent #0E5B37, accent hover #0A4529, accent wash #E3EDE5, warning #8A4B12 on #F0E6D8, danger #A03024 on #F4E3DF. No blue, cyan, purple or gradients anywhere.

SEMANTIC COLOR: warning and danger appear only as a 6px dot plus 12px text, never as a filled chip.

AVOID: pure white #FFFFFF backgrounds, electric mint or neon green, glowing or blurred lines, bloom, glassmorphism, 24px rounded cards, colored status pills, stacked feature cards, three-column grids, section background bands, dividers between bands, drop shadows as hierarchy, oversized decorative numerals other than the three 10px mono numbers, emoji, mock browser windows, watermark, garbled text.

TEXT DISCIPLINE (critical for a 4200px image): only the copy listed above may appear. Inside the product card keep at most twelve short Chinese labels; if any small text cannot be rendered crisply, draw the row as a 1px hairline plus a 4%-black bar instead of inventing glyphs. A clean hairline row is always better than fake text.

MOOD: a Swiss technical monograph printed on uncoated stock — one drawn line crossing four quiet rooms, with a product sheet laid into the first.
