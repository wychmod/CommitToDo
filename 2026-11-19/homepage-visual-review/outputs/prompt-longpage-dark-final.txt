A single very tall vertical artboard for a developer-tool homepage: 1512×4200 px, aspect 1:2.78, read top to bottom as four stacked bands. Dark edition, "branch as architecture". Flat UI comp, crisp 2x detail, no browser chrome, no device mockup, no watermark, no visible section dividers. This brief is complete and self-contained.

GLOBAL
- Canvas #08090A, 4% film grain, no haze, no vignette.
- Left editorial margin 72px; right content edge 1440px.
- One continuous 1px filament travels the entire 4200px height. It is NOT self-luminous: matte anodized graphite with a cool specular edge, lit only near the single node. Stroke 0.75–1px. Never thicker.
- Hierarchy comes from value, stroke weight and type scale only: no cards, no shadows, no glow, no glass. Section separation is achieved by 96px of empty canvas, a 10px mono number, and the line's own 90° turns — nothing else.
- Type scale is fixed in absolute pixels and must NOT scale with the canvas height: display 148, section display 64, section heading 28, body 15, mono 10. Chinese display face with squarish terminals (MiSans-like); technical mono for numbers and hashes.
- Only one accent: mint #6BFFA0, used as position or state only. All other color is graphite, bone white and grey.
- No vertical stretching of elements. Bands are composed, not stretched.

- CTA at x=72, y=584: text only, 15px bone white, preceded by a 40px 1px rule and an arrow. No filled pill anywhere on the page.
- Filament: enters at (0,818), runs as a trunk to (498,703), splits into two suspension arcs opening to a 26% band-height gap, and reconverges at (1074,361) — the single glowing node of the whole page: 20px sphere, machined titanium collar, 2px white-hot core, 60px falloff, three concentric 1px ripple rings at 5% white.

PALETTE: canvas #08090A, panel #0C0E0D, borders #232724, primary text #F2F3EE, secondary #B9BFB9, tertiary #7C837D, accent #6BFFA0, solid accent #34C46E, warning #C08A3E, danger #D0605A. No blue, purple or cyan anywhere.

AVOID: stacked feature cards, three-column grids, section background bands, dividers between bands, glassmorphism, drop shadows as hierarchy, uniformly glowing filaments, particle dust, oversized decorative numerals other than the three 10px mono numbers, pill buttons, colored status chips, emoji, mock browser windows, watermark, garbled text.

TEXT DISCIPLINE (critical for a 4200px image): only the copy listed above may appear. Inside the product window keep at most twelve short Chinese labels; if any small text cannot be rendered crisply, draw the row as a hairline plus a 3%-white bar instead of inventing glyphs. A clean hairline row is always better than fake text.

MOOD: one drawn line crossing four quiet rooms — a technical monograph, not a landing-page template.
