A high-end website homepage visual comp, light edition, "paper and ink", single flat UI artboard, 16:10 landscape, 1512×950, crisp 2x detail, no browser chrome, no device mockup, no watermark.

CONCEPT: an editorial print page about engineering. Warm paper, ink-black type, one drawn line that behaves like a plotter trace, and one product sheet laid on top of it. This is not a reversed dark page; it is a different visual language.

CANVAS & COMPOSITION
- Paper #F4F1E9 (warm neutral, not pure white), 4% fine paper-fiber grain, and a barely visible 2% warm falloff toward the bottom-left corner. No other gradients.
- Editorial 60/40 split. One full-height 1px ink column rule at x=60%, color #1A1C19 at 55% opacity, running from the top edge to the bottom edge. It is a printed column rule, never a UI divider, and it is not rounded or blurred.
- The branch is drawn as a plotter trace: it enters at x=0, y=82%, runs as a single trunk to x=30%, y=70%, splits into two catenary arcs that open into a lens with a 22% frame-height gap at x=48%, and converges at x=64%, y=42% into a solid ink-green node. Both arcs cross the vertical column rule; the lower arc crosses exactly at the node.
- The upper arc passes behind the last line of the headline, so the line is visibly threaded through the typography.

LINES — ink on paper
- Primary arc: 1.25px solid #161815 with a 0.6px lighter core #3A3D38, suggesting a pen groove. No glow, no blur, no halo.
- The far arc drops to 45% opacity to imply depth; nothing else is transparent.
- Secondary hairlines: 1px #C9C6BC.
- The node at the merge: a 16px solid disc of deep ink green #0E5B37 with a 3px paper-white knockout collar, and nothing else. No glow, no ring, no shadow.

TYPOGRAPHY — the black mass comes from type
- Wordmark top-left at 14px / 700 ink; four nav items at 12px #4A5049. No button in the header.
- Eyebrow at x=72, y=124: 10px mono, letter-spacing 0.24em, #6E726A: "COMMITTODO — BRANCH-BASED TO-DO".
- Headline at x=72, y=162: Chinese display type at 156px, weight 800, line-height 0.88, letter-spacing -0.035em, ink #101210. Line 1 "把目标分支化". Line 2 "把进展提交掉" with the two characters "提交" in deep green #0E5B37. Set the second line to overhang the vertical column rule, so the type physically breaks the grid.
- Sub-copy at x=72, y=500: 17px / 1.6, max width 380, #4A5049: "一条主干，随时分叉，做完就合并。每次推进，都留在时间线上。"
- One CTA at x=72, y=574: a solid ink #101210 rectangle, 50px tall, 0 radius, paper-white 15px text "开始第一条分支 →". This is the only filled button on the paper.
- A mono footnote at x=72, y=878: 10px #8A8D84, letter-spacing 0.16em: "LOCAL-FIRST · NO ACCOUNT · INDEXEDDB".

PRODUCT WINDOW — a white sheet with an ink cap
- A paper-white card #FBFAF6 entering from the right edge at x=56%, y=46%, cropped by the bottom and right edges. 3px corner radius (print-like, almost square), 1px #161815 border, and one directional shadow only: 0 24px 48px -32px rgba(16,18,16,0.28). No ambient glow, no blur halo.
- A 36px solid ink #101210 strip runs across the top of the card, carrying a 10px paper-white mono label "CommitToDo — 分支视图" at the left and a single 1px paper-white hairline under it. This strip is the only dark block on the page besides the type.
- Inside, two panes only, drawn like printed UI. Left: a vertical branch tree, 3 rows at 15px ink, hairline connectors #C9C6BC, one 6px deep-green active square. Right: 3 task rows at 14px, and below them a vertical commit timeline of four entries with 12px mono hashes and 11px timestamps, separated by 1px #E4E1D8 rules.
- All UI text minimum 12px, sharp, correctly spelled, real Chinese copy, no lorem ipsum, no fake glyphs.

WHY IT STILL HAS IMPACT ON PAPER
- Extreme scale: a 156px headline against 10px mono labels, a 15x ratio.
- Extreme emptiness: the paper below the sub-copy stays blank until the footnote rule. No cards, no icons, no illustrations, no logo strip.
- One black mass: the headline itself, plus the 36px ink cap on the card.
- One saturated color: deep green on exactly two characters, one node, one active square.
- Print texture: 4% fiber grain and the full-height column rule make it read as a printed monograph, not a SaaS template.

PALETTE: paper #F4F1E9, ink #101210, secondary text #4A5049, hairline #C9C6BC, card #FBFAF6, one deep green #0E5B37. No blue, no cyan, no purple, no gradients.

AVOID: pure white #FFFFFF background, electric mint or neon green (it disappears on paper), glowing or blurred lines, glassmorphism, 24px rounded cards, colorful status chips, icon rows, three-column feature grids, "trusted by" logo strips, gradient blobs, drop shadows on every element, watermark, garbled text.

MOOD: a Swiss technical monograph printed on uncoated stock, with one product sheet laid into the spread.
