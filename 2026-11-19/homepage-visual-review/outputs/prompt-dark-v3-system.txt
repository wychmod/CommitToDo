A high-end website homepage visual comp for a design system that must scale from a hero to a dense working app. Dark edition, "branch as architecture", single flat UI artboard, 16:10 landscape, 1512×950, crisp 2x detail, no browser chrome, no device mockup, no watermark.

THE SYSTEM — this artboard must prove a language that still works at 40px row height:
1. Hierarchy is carried by value, stroke weight and type scale only — never by glow, blur or drop shadow. Exactly one light source exists on the page (the merge node). In UI, the same idea degrades to a single 1px lit top edge on panels.
2. One accent: mint #6BFFA0 appears strictly as position or state — the active node, one active row indicator, one keyword. Everything else is graphite and bone.
3. One structural motif: a 1px line. At hero scale it is a physical filament; on a section it is a full-bleed rule; in a list a row rule; in a tree a connector; in a timeline a spine; on a focused input a lit edge. Same element at six scales.
4. Only three line weights exist: 0.75px hairline, 1px default, 1.5px emphasis. Nothing thicker.
5. Surfaces are matte and flat, two tiers only: canvas #08090A and panel #0C0E0D, separated by a 1px #232724 border. No elevation tiers, no ambient shadow, no glass except one 6px material sliver.
6. Controls are 36–40px tall, 2px radius, 1px border, no fill. Exactly one solid action per screen.

HERO COMPOSITION (marketing scale only)
- Near-black graphite #08090A, 5% film grain, no haze, no vignette wash.
- Asymmetric 62/38 split: the left 62% is the editorial column, the right 38% is the band where the product window enters, cropped by the bottom and right edges.
- The filament enters at x=0, y=86%, runs as a trunk to x=33%, y=74%, splits into two suspension-bridge arcs that open into a lens with a 26% frame-height gap at x=54%, and reconverges at x=71%, y=38% into the node. The fork and the merge must be legible as geometry at a glance.
- The upper arc passes behind the last line of the headline at 10% opacity, so type and structure sit in different depth planes.

FILAMENT
- 0.75–1px, matte anodized graphite with a cool specular edge. It must NOT be self-luminous: only within 25% of the node does it carry a mint highlight, then fade to 12% white, then die into black.
- Shallow depth of field: far arc 3px out of focus, near arc tack sharp.
- Exactly one 90px-long motion streak on the lower arc, 30px before the node. No dust, no particles, no sparkles.

NODE — the only light source in the frame
- 20px sphere seated in a machined titanium collar, 2px white-hot core, controlled 60px falloff, three concentric 1px ripple rings at 5% white.
- No orbital ring, no atom loop, no lens flare. In the product UI this reduces to a 6px mint square status dot.

TYPOGRAPHY — a scale that survives density
- Scale: display 148 / 96 (marketing only), H1 40, H2 28, H3 20, body 17 marketing / 15 app, secondary 13, caption 12, mono 11–12 for hashes, timestamps and labels. Chinese display face with squarish terminals (MiSans-like), technical mono for data.
- Wordmark top-left 14px / 600 bone white; four nav items 12px #6E746F. No button in the header.
- Eyebrow at x=72, y=128: 10px mono, 0.22em tracking, #6E746F: "COMMITTODO — BRANCH-BASED TO-DO".
- Headline at x=72, y=170: 148px, weight 700, line-height 0.90, tracking -0.03em, #F2F3EE. Line 1 "把目标分支化". Line 2 "把进展提交掉" with "提交" in #6BFFA0. The second line overhangs the left edge of the product window.
- Sub-copy at x=72, y=506: 17px / 1.6, max width 400, #8B918B: "一条主干，随时分叉，做完就合并。每次推进，都留在时间线上。"
- CTA at x=72, y=584: text only, 15px bone white, followed by a 40px 1px rule and an arrow. No filled capsule anywhere in the frame.

PRODUCT WINDOW — must read as a real dense app, not a poster prop
- Matte plate #0C0E0D at x=62%, y=46%, cropped by the bottom and right edges; 1px #232724 border, 2px radius, one 1px specular highlight along the top edge fading after 45% of its width, plus one 6px glass sliver holding a dim mint reflection.
- A 36px header strip with a 12px label and a 1px rule.
- Two panes only. Left: branch tree with 40px rows, 15px labels, 6px mint square status dots, 1px connectors, 14px indent per level. Right: four task rows at 40px with 14px text, then a commit timeline of four entries with a 1px spine, 11px mono hashes and 12px timestamps.
- Show two states so the system is proven: one focused input (40px tall, 1px mint border, no glow) and one selected row (1px mint left edge, 6% white wash).
- All UI text minimum 12px, sharp, correctly spelled, real Chinese copy, no lorem ipsum, no fake glyphs.

SEMANTIC COLOR — the only other hues allowed, always as a 6px dot plus 12px text, never as a filled chip: warning #C08A3E, danger #D0605A. Success uses the brand mint.

EDITORIAL LAYER (marketing only, must be removable without breaking the page): a 3% white 96px measurement grid clipped to the upper-right quadrant, and one 480px numeral "01" at 2% white positioned so the node overlaps its counter.

PALETTE: canvas #08090A, panel #0C0E0D, border #232724, primary text #F2F3EE, secondary #B9BFB9, tertiary #7C837D, disabled #4E544F, accent #6BFFA0 / solid #34C46E / wash #10301C. No blue, purple or cyan anywhere.

LIGHT: one key (the node), one cool titanium rim on the window's top edge, everything else in shadow. Blacks truly black, no grey haze.

MATERIALS: matte black anodized plate, brushed titanium collar, 0.75px etched filament, 5% grain, exactly one glass sliver.

AVOID: thick neon tubes, uniform glow along every line, atom or orbit rings, lens flare, glow haze, particle dust, two filled pills, five-column dashboard walls, 12px rounded cards, gradient buttons, colored pills for status, drop shadows as hierarchy, watermark, garbled text.

MOOD: a technical drawing of a suspension bridge lit by a single lamp — quiet, exact, expensive, and built to be inhabited rather than admired.
